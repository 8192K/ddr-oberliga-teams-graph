How have the 22 teams in the GDR top flight of football ("Oberliga") of the 1980s evolved over time?

This interactive graph shows their development. Use a mouse or touchpad/touchscreen to navigate. You can highlight up to three teams permanently. Use ctrl+mouse wheel, touch or buttons for zoom.
